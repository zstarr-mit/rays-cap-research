Layout for data/research_outputs/
===================================

core/
  Normalized events (master_events_normalized.csv), eligible list,
  enrichment universes (hk_only / all_symbols / combined), summary_event_*.csv

diagnostics/
  Validation and QA: diag_*.csv, master_*_audit.csv (parse, duplicates, primary symbol, etc.)

coverage/
  yfinance_download_audit*.csv, price_coverage_*.csv, price_missing_*.csv

features/
  event_features_enriched.csv, event_feature_audit.csv — pre-event market features per index row

event_study/
  event_windows_daily.csv, event_study_agg_*.csv, event_study_pre_post_summary.csv

insights/
  insight_*.csv, key_findings.txt — descriptive comparisons from the pipeline

charts/
  PNG figures from hk_event_research_pipeline.py

candidate_panels/
  candidate_panel_*.csv, candidate_stock_date_panel.csv — stock×date panels for horizon labels

ml_baseline/
  Binary inclusion model: train_table_snapshot, metrics report, walk-forward outputs

ml_market_liquidity_baseline/
  Market liquidity baseline artifacts

review_cycle/
  Option A review-batch labels + asof dates (build_review_cycle_panel.py)

stock_connect_fundamental_trends/
  yfinance fundamentals experiments for Stock Connect events

Run hk_event_research_pipeline.py to regenerate core → insights outputs in these folders.
